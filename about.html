The kitchen

Uintah Valley LLC

Here at Uintah Valley we make homemade goods in Utah. Vanilla Extract is the main product we bottle today.

This is an in home operation with grander hopes and dreams. We want to provide the world with something the other guys won't: actually PURE Vanilla Extract! Our current extract product is made with Madagascar vanilla beans steeped in locally distilled spirits.

We take the label "Pure" seriously. We do not add water to stretch the extract; we do not add sugar or high-fructose corn syrup to sweeten it up. We do not use chemical flavor components or beaver butt paste! (It's a real thing! It's called "castoreum.") We let the beans and extractives have their say in the flavor, and we rely on the product to speak for itself!

If you want the best Vanilla Extract on the planet, or if you have a question about a batch or a label, write us!

Currently we can only sell to Utah residents
