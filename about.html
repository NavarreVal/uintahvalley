The kitchen

Uintah Valley LLC

Uintah Valley is a Utah limited liability company. The name is for the valley — a trail-marker badge more than a tech logo. We make homemade goods from a small kitchen in Utah. Vanilla is the line we bottle today.

This is a kitchen operation, not a national brand story. The current extract is Madagascar vanilla beans steeped in locally distilled spirits. We are not listing a biography, awards, or a founding myth here.

The integrity piece is short: we do not add water to stretch extract. We do not add sugar or high-fructose corn syrup. We do not use chemical flavor components or castoreum. That is a kitchen choice, not a lab certificate.

Mainline Pure Vanilla Extract is out of stock indefinitely. If you have a question about a batch or a label, write us.

What we will not claim

  • No fake reviews or star ratings.
  • No USDA / organic / fair-trade seals we have not earned on this page.
  • No guaranteed ship dates or invented warehouse inventory.
