The maker

Uintah Valley LLC

Uintah Valley is a Utah limited liability company named for the Uintah Basin — mountains, sage, and a badge that looks like a trail marker more than a tech logo. We make homemade goods from a small kitchen in Utah. Vanilla is the line we bottle today.

This is a kitchen operation, not a national brand story. The current extract is Madagascar vanilla beans steeped in alcohol. We are not listing a biography, awards, or a founding myth here, and we are not previewing other products that are not on the shelf.

The integrity piece is short: we do not add water to stretch extract. We do not add sugar or high-fructose corn syrup. We do not use chemical flavor components or castoreum. That is a kitchen choice, not a lab certificate — this page does not invent tests we have not run.

Mainline Pure Vanilla Extract is out of stock indefinitely. If you want a bottle now, request an Experimental trial. If you have a question about a batch or a label, write us.
