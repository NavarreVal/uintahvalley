The maker

Uintah Valley LLC

Uintah Valley is a Utah limited liability company that bottles homemade vanilla extract. The name points at the Uintah Basin — mountains, sage, and a badge that looks like a trail marker more than a tech logo.

This is a small kitchen operation, not a national brand story. Extract is made by steeping Madagascar vanilla beans in alcohol and waiting. We are not listing a biography, awards, or a founding myth here.

The integrity piece is short: we do not add water to stretch extract. We do not add sugar or high-fructose corn syrup. We do not use chemical flavor components or castoreum. That is a kitchen choice, not a lab certificate — this page does not invent tests we have not run.

Photographed bottles wear 1 fl oz labels. An earlier bottling note on the side of those labels is left off the homepage on purpose.

Mainline Pure Vanilla Extract is out of stock indefinitely. If you want a bottle now, request an Experimental trial. If you have a question about a batch or a label, write us.
