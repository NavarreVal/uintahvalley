What’s in the bottle

Why Uintah Valley

We do not pad a jar to hit a price. The extract is beans in alcohol. That is the whole argument.

No water
Many extracts add water to stretch the bottle. We do not. Ours is not watered down.
Two ingredients
Alcohol and vanilla beans. That is the recipe. Nothing else goes in to make the extract.
No castoreum
No castoreum — the animal secretion sometimes sold as a flavor note, and in plain talk, beaver butt paste. Not in our kitchen.
No chemical flavor
No lab-made vanilla flavor components. The taste comes from beans sitting in spirit.
No added sugar
No added sugars and no high-fructose corn syrup. Some grocery extracts use both. We do not.
