Questions

Why only Utah?

Currently we can only sell to Utah residents. This is a homemade food product for home use, not for resale, processed and prepared without state or local inspection, under Utah's cottage food program. If that's not you, reach out anyways and we'll see what we can do for you.

Do I pay on this site?

No. There is no live checkout, and we won't ask for your payment info here. Add items to a request list, then email the request from the Request List page. We reply with availability, a confirmed total, and how to pay. Payment and confirmation is arranged off-site.

Does it contain alcohol?

Yes. The extract contains alcohol, which is the best extractive, and we don't water it down. It is made with locally distilled spirits and imported vanilla beans.

What's in it?

No water, no chemical flavors, no added sugar or HFCS, and never castoreum. Locally distilled spirits and high quality vanilla. Nothing else goes in to make the extract.

Can I order the mainline Pure Vanilla Extract?

Mainline Pure Vanilla Extract is sold out for now. It cannot be ordered here. Other items can still be requested.

Are the prices final?

Prices are provisional. Sending a request is not a paid order. We reply with a confirmed total.
