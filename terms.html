Questions

Why only Utah?

Currently we can only sell to Utah residents. This is a homemade food product for home use, not for resale, processed and prepared without state or local inspection, under Utah's cottage food program. If that's not you, reach out anyway and we'll see what we can do for you.

Do I pay on this site?

No. There is no live checkout, and we won't ask for your payment info here. Add items to a request list, then email the request from the Request List page. We reply with availability, a confirmed total, and how to pay. Payment and confirmation is arranged off-site.

Are the prices final?

Prices are provisional. Sending a request is not a paid order. A request is not a purchase. We reply with a confirmed total.
